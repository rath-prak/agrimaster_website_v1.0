About Agri Master

Cambodian-owned animal nutrition
built for local farmers

Established in 2004 as part of Medivet Cambodia, Agri Master serves all major segments of Cambodia's animal feed sector — and was the first Cambodian company to commercially produce floating fish feed.

Our story
ANIMAL NUTRITION

Stage of Production

We produce a comprehensive range of high-quality animal feeds for swine, poultry, ruminants and aquaculture. Our feeds are carefully formulated to meet the nutritional needs of different animal species at every stage of production, supporting healthy growth, development and productivity.

Milestones

Two decades of growth

  1. 2004

    Cambodia's first locally owned and operated animal feed producers.

  2. 2013

    Opened a modern feed mill in Kandal Province near Phnom Penh.

  3. 2020

    Cambodia's first aquafeed plant, with a capacity of 1,500 tons/month.

  4. 2023–2026

    Expanding and modernising the facility, including new silos with 10,000 tons of storage capacity.

RAW MATERIALS

Quality animal nutrition starts with high quality ingredients.

Agri-Master prioritises locally sourced agricultural products while importing high-quality raw materials to produce nutritious, consistent feed for Cambodia’s livestock and aquaculture industries.

Our sourcing approach supports Cambodian agriculture while providing access to quality ingredients from international markets. All raw materials undergo strict in-house quality control to ensure nutritional value, safety, and consistency.

Supporting Cambodian Farmers

We prioritise purchasing locally grown corn, cassava, broken rice, and rice bran whenever available, supporting Cambodian farmers and the local agricultural economy.

High quality International suppliers

We source high-quality raw materials from international suppliers, primarily in the United States, to complement local ingredients and support our animal nutrition formulations.
