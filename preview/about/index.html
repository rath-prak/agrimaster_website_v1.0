Proudly Cambodian. Committed to Agriculture.

Since 2004, Agri Master has produced animal feed for Cambodia's livestock and aquaculture sectors, combining quality manufacturing with a commitment to local farmers and agricultural development.

Company Story

Who We Are

Established in 2004 as part of Medivet Cambodia, Agri Master is one of the country's pioneering locally owned animal feed manufacturers.

Our production facility in Kandal Province supplies feed for swine, poultry, ruminants and freshwater aquaculture.

Our Journey

  1. 2004

    Agri Master was established.

  2. 2013

    Opened a modern feed mill in Kandal Province.

  3. 2020

    Established Cambodia's first commercial aquafeed plant.

  4. Today

    Continuing to serve Cambodia's livestock and aquaculture producers.

Our Purpose

Supporting Farmers. Strengthening Cambodia.

We believe quality animal nutrition supports healthy livestock, productive farming and the long-term development of Cambodia's food and agricultural sector.

Ingredients

Quality Begins with Ingredients

Good feed begins with good raw materials.

We prioritise suitable ingredients grown in Cambodia when available and source dependable international supplies when needed. Our focus is nutritional quality, safety, consistency and reliable supply.

Quality

Quality from Ingredient to Finished Feed

Our documented quality-control process includes raw-material inspection, production monitoring, laboratory analysis and finished-product checks. Each stage contributes to the consistency and reliability of our animal feed.

Technical Relationships

Local Experience. International Knowledge.

Agri Master's longstanding relationship with the American Soybean Association's World Initiative for Soy in Human Health (ASA/WISHH) has supported technical knowledge exchange in feed formulation, production practices and aquaculture development.

Cambodia Commitment

Our Commitment to Cambodia

Our contribution extends beyond the factory. Through local feed manufacturing, purchasing suitable Cambodian agricultural materials and supporting the exchange of technical knowledge, we aim to strengthen connections across Cambodia's agricultural sector.
