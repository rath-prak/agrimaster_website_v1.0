Proudly Cambodian. Committed to Agriculture.

Since 2004, Agri Master has grown alongside Cambodia's livestock and aquaculture industries, providing quality animal nutrition while supporting the development of local agriculture.

Who We Are

Agri Master was founded in 2004 as part of Medivet Cambodia, becoming one of the country's pioneering locally owned animal feed manufacturers.

Today, our modern manufacturing facility in Kandal Province produces feed for swine, poultry, ruminants and freshwater aquaculture.

We combine high-quality ingredients, carefully developed formulations, modern production technology and practical expertise to meet the evolving needs of Cambodian farmers.

Our Journey
  1. 2004

    Our Beginning

    Agri Master was established as a Cambodian-owned animal feed producer.

  3. 2020

  4. Today

    Growing with Cambodia

    Continuing to develop our production capabilities, product portfolio and agricultural partnerships.

Our Purpose

Supporting Farmers. Strengthening Cambodia.

We believe quality animal nutrition plays an important role in healthy livestock, productive farms and Cambodia's food security.

Our purpose is to support farmers through reliable feed, practical knowledge and a long-term commitment to the development of Cambodia's livestock and aquaculture industries.

Quality at Every Stage

From Raw Materials to Finished Feed

Good animal feed begins with good ingredients.

We prioritise suitable Cambodian raw materials where available and source high-quality ingredients internationally when needed.

Our manufacturing process incorporates raw-material inspection, production monitoring, laboratory analysis and finished-product quality checks to maintain consistent feed standards.

Technical Expertise and Partnerships

Local Knowledge. International Cooperation.

We believe agricultural progress is strengthened through knowledge-sharing and collaboration.

Our longstanding relationship with the American Soybean Association's World Initiative for Soy in Human Health (ASA/WISHH) has supported technical knowledge exchange in feed formulation, production and aquaculture development.

These relationships complement our understanding of Cambodian farming conditions and our commitment to practical animal nutrition.

Our Commitment to Cambodia

Growing Together for a Stronger Future

Our commitment extends beyond animal feed manufacturing.

By prioritising suitable locally grown agricultural ingredients, supporting farmer education and working with industry partners, we aim to create opportunities across Cambodia's agricultural value chain.

We believe the growth of Agri Master should also contribute to the farmers, businesses and communities that support Cambodia's agricultural future.
