Animal Feed for Every Stage of Production

Agri Master provides high-quality complete feeds and selected concentrates for Cambodia's livestock and aquaculture industries.

Our products are formulated for different animal species and stages of production, supporting healthy growth, animal performance and farm productivity.

Feed Types

Different Forms for Different Feeding Needs

Agri Master produces animal feed in several forms, depending on the animal species and product requirements.

Mash Feed
Finely ground and mixed ingredients, formulated for specific feeding requirements.
Pellet Feed
Compressed feed pellets designed for convenient handling and feeding.
Crumble Feed
Smaller particles produced from pellets, suitable for selected young animals.
Floating Aquafeed
Specialised floating pellets designed for freshwater aquaculture.

We also offer selected feed concentrates intended for mixing with other ingredients according to product instructions.

Our Feed Brands

Trusted Animal Nutrition. Made in Cambodia.

Our portfolio includes Master Feed, Super Feed, Eco Feed, Vital Feed, Golden Feed and Diamond Feed.

Master Feed is our original and most established brand, while newer additions such as Golden Feed and Diamond Feed have expanded our product portfolio.

Each brand reflects Agri Master's commitment to quality animal nutrition and the needs of Cambodian farmers.
